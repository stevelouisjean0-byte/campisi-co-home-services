Before you ask

What makes an enquiry easy to answer.

  • Send the whole list. Several jobs in one visit is how the reviews describe this working — one customer had "a couple of house projects" and he "tackled them all". Five small things at once beats five separate visits.
  • Photographs help more than paragraphs. A picture of the wall, the fixture or the box it came in usually decides what needs bringing, and often removes a first visit entirely.
  • Say what is already bought. Fandeliers, mirrors and rods are often sitting in a hallway waiting. Knowing the model matters for the fixing behind it.
  • Mention who sent you. Most people here came from a neighbour or a friend. It is worth saying — it is how this business has always worked.

Questions

Straight answers.

Is there a phone number?

Not one published on the Google listing at present, which is why this page routes enquiries through the listing itself rather than printing a number that might be wrong. If you have one from a neighbour who has used him, that will reach him fastest.

What kind of jobs are worth calling about?

Reviewers describe hanging curtain rods, fandeliers and mirrors, plus kitchen and bathroom work and multi-item project lists. One calls him "truly a jack of all trades." See the work for the full picture.

How far ahead should I ask?

Ask and find out — punctuality is one of the four counted topics in the reviews, so the answer you get is likely to be the one that happens.

Where does he work?

The listing does not publish a service area, so ask directly with your address. Two of the three visible reviews came through neighbourhood referrals, which suggests the work clusters locally.

Is he licensed and insured?

Ask on the enquiry and get the current details directly — this site does not print licence numbers that have not been verified against the Florida DBPR record.
