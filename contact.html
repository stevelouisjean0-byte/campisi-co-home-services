Before you ask

What makes an enquiry easy to answer.

  • Send the whole list. Several jobs in one visit is how the reviews describe this working — one customer had "a couple of house projects" and he "tackled them all". Five small things at once beats five separate visits.
  • Photographs help more than paragraphs. A picture of the wall, the fixture or the box it came in usually decides what needs bringing, and often removes a first visit entirely.
  • Say what is already bought. Fandeliers, mirrors and rods are often sitting in a hallway waiting. Knowing the model matters for the fixing behind it.
  • Mention who sent you. Most people here came from a neighbour or a friend. It is worth saying — it is how this business has always worked.
