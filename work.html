The work

Everything on the list, done once.

One reviewer called Anthony "truly a jack of all trades," another gave a partial inventory of a single visit. What follows is drawn from those descriptions rather than from a brochure.

  1. Hanging & mounting

    Curtain rods, mirrors, shelving, art and televisions. The whole job is in the parts you cannot see afterwards: finding the stud or setting the right anchor, getting the height right for the room rather than for the instructions, and leaving it dead level.

    A mirror hung badly is obvious from across the room forever. This is the category the reviews name most specifically.

    Koko U. — curtain rods, mirrors

  2. Fixtures & fandeliers

    Ceiling fans, light fixtures, and the fan-chandelier combinations that reviewers call fandeliers. These are heavier and less forgiving than a plain fixture — they need a fan-rated box behind them, properly balanced blades, and a switch arrangement that makes sense once it is up.

    Koko U. — fandeliers

  3. Kitchens

    Kitchen is one of the photo categories on the Google listing, which means there is finished kitchen work worth showing. Cabinet hardware, fittings, and the small alterations that make a kitchen work rather than merely look finished.

    Listing photo category

  4. Bathrooms

    Also a listing photo category. Bathrooms punish shortcuts more than any other room, and they are where "attention to detail" — one of the four counted topics — earns its place in a review.

    Listing photo category

  5. Project lists

    "I had a couple of house projects and he tackled them all neatly and efficiently." Batching several jobs into one visit is the sensible way to use a good tradesperson, and both detailed reviews describe it working that way.

    Koko U. · Lisa M

  6. Exterior

    Exterior appears in the listing's photo categories, so there is outdoor work in the portfolio. The written reviews describe interior projects in detail and exterior only by implication — so confirm the scope directly rather than taking this page's word for it.

    Listing photo category · confirm
